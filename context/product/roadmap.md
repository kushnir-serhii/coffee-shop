# Product Roadmap: Meridian — Coffee & Equipment

_This roadmap outlines our strategic direction based on customer needs and business goals. It focuses on the "what" and "why," not the technical "how."_

---

### Phase 1

_The highest priority features that form the core foundation of the product._

- [x] **Two-Lane Storefront**
  - [x] **Editorial Homepage:** An editorial hero and lane split so visitors see straight away that Meridian sells both coffee and equipment, followed by featured beans, an equipment row, sourcing and a subscription prompt.
  - [x] **Coffee Catalog:** Let home brewers filter beans by roast, process and brew method, and sort them, to find a lot that matches their taste.
  - [x] **Equipment Catalog:** Let gear buyers browse by category and sort by price.

- [x] **Product Pages for Two Kinds of Purchase**
  - [x] **Sensory-First Coffee Pages:** Origin story, tasting notes, roast meter, flavour profile, and grind and bag-size options, so an emotional, repeat purchase feels easy.
  - [x] **Spec-First Equipment Pages:** Specification table, finish switcher and comparison table, so a researched, high-price purchase feels safe.
  - [x] **Interactive 3D Grinder:** A rotatable view of the flagship Atlas E1 on desktop, with an image fallback elsewhere.

- [x] **Buying Flow**
  - [x] **Coffee Subscription:** A configurator for weekly, fortnightly or monthly deliveries at 10–15% off, with a clear per-delivery price.
  - [x] **Shared Cart:** One slide-over cart for coffee and equipment, kept between visits.
  - [x] **Demo Checkout:** A three-step checkout (contact, delivery, payment) ending in an order confirmation. No payment is taken.

- [x] **Brand & Trust**
  - [x] **About Page:** Story, sourcing report, roastery and contact.
  - [x] **Legal Pages:** Terms, privacy and shipping.

---

### Phase 2

_Once the foundational features are complete, we will move on to these high-value additions._

- [ ] **Real Photography Pass** _(in progress)_
  - [x] **Coffee and Editorial Photos:** Real bag shots, gallery images and editorial images replace the placeholders across the homepage, catalogs, product pages, cart and checkout.
  - [ ] **Complete Equipment Finishes:** Photograph the 7 finishes that still show a placeholder, so buyers always see the exact finish they will receive.
  - [ ] **Distinct Gallery per Coffee:** Give each lot its own beans, brew and origin photos instead of shared shots, so each coffee feels like its own story.
  - [ ] **Reshoot Off-Brief Images:** Replace the About roastery photo (it shows another brand) and lighten the home hero to match the art direction.
  - [ ] **Subscription Image in Cart:** Show a product image for subscriptions in the cart and order summary, like every other line.
  - [ ] **Polished Placeholders:** Restore the coloured placeholder style for any slot that is still waiting on a photo.

- [ ] **Shareable Portfolio Link**
  - [ ] **Social Share Card:** A branded preview image when the Meridian link is shared in messages, LinkedIn or a portfolio, which matters most for the portfolio audience.

- [ ] **Graceful Dead Ends**
  - [ ] **Branded 404 Page:** A page-not-found screen in Meridian's style that points visitors back to the two lanes, instead of the default framework page.
  - [ ] **Friendly Error & Loading States:** On-brand screens for errors and slow loads, so the store never looks broken.

---

### Phase 3

_Features planned for future consideration. Their priority and scope may be refined based on user feedback from earlier phases._

- [ ] **Proven Quality Bar**
  - [ ] **Verified Accessibility & Performance:** Confirm every page meets WCAG 2.1 AA and scores 90+ on mobile Lighthouse Performance, and fix what falls short.
  - [ ] **Key-Journey Checks:** Automatic checks that the coffee path, equipment path, subscription and checkout keep working after every change.
  - [ ] **Usability Testing:** Run informal tests against the success metrics: lane clarity in 10 seconds, checkout in under 2 minutes, subscription price understood.

- [ ] **Ukrainian Market** _(planned for a later version)_
  - [ ] **Ukrainian Language:** All pages and product content available in Ukrainian, reflecting the Lviv roastery.
  - [ ] **UAH Pricing:** Show prices in hryvnia for Ukrainian visitors.

- [ ] **Richer Equipment Showcase**
  - [ ] **Photoreal 3D Grinder:** Replace the hand-built grinder model with a detailed, photoreal one.
